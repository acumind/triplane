Dispatch within 24 h on business days from Bengaluru and Bhiwandi warehouses.

| Zone | Coverage | Delivery TAT | COD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone A — metros | 110xxx, 400xxx, 560xxx, 600xxx, 500xxx, 700xxx | 2–3 days | Yes |
| Zone B — tier-2 | ~14,000 pincodes | 3–5 days | Yes |
| Zone C — ODA/remote | NE states, J&K, A&N, Lakshadweep, hill ODA | 6–10 days | No |

COD limit **₹20,000 per order**; RO-7 orders to Zone C are prepaid-only and carry a
₹499 ODA freight surcharge shown at checkout. Appliances above 8 kg ship surface,
not air — expedited shipping is not offered on the RO-7.

**Serviceability ≠ installability:** a pincode can be deliverable while
[[installation-and-demo]] for the RO-7 is not yet available there; in that case the
storefront blocks the RO-7 SKU even though the mixer and cooktop remain orderable.
Agents must never promise installation from delivery serviceability alone.
