Dhruva services through **142 ASCs across 96 cities** plus doorstep technicians in
Zone A/B pincodes. An ASC visit or carry-in is *authorized* only when booked through
[[warranty-claim-process]] (toll-free 1800-419-xxxx, WhatsApp, or the storefront
service page) and a **service request (SR) number** is issued — walk-ins without an
SR are re-routed through booking.

Any opening of the unit by a non-ASC technician — including local mixer-repair
shops re-winding motors — **voids the remaining warranty** for that unit, per
[[warranty-policy]]. ASCs stock fast-moving parts (couplers, jar blade assemblies,
RO pumps, IC-1800 IGBT boards); other parts arrive in 3–5 business days against the
SR. Out-of-warranty repairs at ASCs carry a 90-day workmanship guarantee on the
replaced part only.
