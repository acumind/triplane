7-stage wall-mount purifier: sediment → pre-carbon → RO membrane → UV chamber →
post-carbon → mineralizer → copper infusion. 8 L storage, suitable for **input TDS
200–2000 ppm** (borewell and tanker water included).


**Installation is mandatory and free** within serviceable pincodes when booked through
[[installation-and-demo]]; self-installation or third-party plumber installation
voids the leak-related coverage in [[warranty-policy]].

**What the warranty does and does not cover here** is the single most common support
question: the electrical system (pump, SMPS, UV ballast, solenoid) carries the full
product warranty, while **consumables — all filters, the RO membrane, and the copper
cartridge — are excluded** and follow the replacement intervals in
[[ro7-consumable-schedule]]. Membrane life shortens on high-TDS water; running input
above 2000 ppm without a pre-softener is treated as misuse.

Annual maintenance plans ([[dhruva-shield]] AquaCare tier) bundle consumables at a
fixed yearly price with two scheduled services.
