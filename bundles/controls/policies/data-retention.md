Control evidence is retained for seven years from the end of the financial year it relates to, then deleted.

Evidence containing customer identifiers is stored in the restricted evidence vault and is never copied into working documents. Where a control samples customer records — as [[revenue-cutoff]] does — the sample is referenced by identifier, not reproduced.
