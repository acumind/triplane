The **GST tax invoice** is the sole proof of purchase for warranty, returns, and the
40%-of-MRP jar program: it fixes the coverage start date, the invoice value used for
refunds, and ties the product **serial number** to the buyer.

Accepted: Dhruva storefront invoice (auto-emailed, re-downloadable from the account
page or via WhatsApp support with registered mobile + order id), or a marketplace
GST invoice naming the same serial. Not accepted: order-confirmation emails, payment
screenshots, courier slips, or invoices where the serial does not match the unit
(serial plate locations: MG under the base · IC rear label · RO behind the pre-filter
cover).

Lost both invoice and order id? Service centers can look up D2C purchases by
registered mobile number; marketplace purchases cannot be reconstructed by Dhruva —
recover the invoice from the marketplace account first.
