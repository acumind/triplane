## Scope

This policy governs refunds of **installation and demo fees** charged at the time of purchase or checkout, and applies exclusively to two products:

- **[[ro-7]]** — Dhruva RO-7 Water Purifier (RO + UV + Copper)
- **[[ic-1800]]** — Dhruva IC-1800 Induction Cooktop (optional 16 A wall-point service)

For all other return and refund matters, refer to [[returns-policy]].

---

## Fee Reference

| Product | Fee Type | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| RO-7 | Standard installation kit | Free (₹0) within serviceable pincodes |
| RO-7 | Extra tubing (per metre) | ₹60/m (chargeable extra) |
| RO-7 | Booster pump (pressure < 0.3 kg/cm²) | ₹2,950 (chargeable extra) |
| IC-1800 | Optional 16 A wall-point service | ₹349 (metro pincodes only) |

> Fees and service scope are defined in [[installation-and-demo]].

## Refund Eligibility

### RO-7 Installation Fees

| Scenario | Refundable? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Product returned within 10-day window per [[returns-policy]] | ✅ Yes — full refund of any chargeable extras paid | Installation must not have been completed; if completed, see row below |
| Product returned after installation was completed | ✅ Partial — chargeable extras (booster pump, extra tubing) refunded pro-rata; standard kit fee (₹0) not applicable | Return must still be within the 10-day window |
| Customer-cancelled installation slot **before** technician visit | ✅ Yes — full refund of any chargeable extras pre-paid | Cancellation must be raised via SR link before technician dispatch |
| Technician visit failed due to **site not ready** (no drilling permission, no water point) | ❌ No — visit converts to chargeable (₹199) on next booking per [[installation-and-demo]] | No refund on the ₹199 revisit fee once visit is completed |
| Pincode found non-installable after order placement | ✅ Yes — full refund of any installation extras pre-paid | Customer notified within 48 h of delivery; refund initiated automatically |

### IC-1800 Wall-Point Service Fee (₹349)

| Scenario | Refundable? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Service cancelled **before** technician dispatch | ✅ Yes — full refund (₹349) | Cancel via SR link or customer care |
| Service cancelled **after** technician visit completed | ❌ No | Service has been rendered |
| Product returned within 10-day window per [[returns-policy]], service not yet completed | ✅ Yes — full refund (₹349) | Automatically triggered on product return approval |
| Product returned within 10-day window, wall-point service already completed | ❌ No | Service has been rendered; only the product refund is processed per [[returns-policy]] |

---

## Refund Method & Timeline

- Refunds are credited to the **original payment method** (COD orders: UPI/bank transfer).
- Processing time: **5–7 business days** from approval, consistent with [[returns-policy]].
- Digital receipt issued by the technician serves as the proof of payment for any chargeable extras.

---

## Exclusions

- This policy does **not** apply to third-party or self-arranged installation — only to Dhruva-dispatched technicians booked through the official SR flow in [[installation-and-demo]].
- Chargeable extras for RO-7 (e.g., booster pump ₹2,950) paid directly to the technician are non-refundable once the component is fitted, unless the product itself is returned within the 10-day window.
- Marketplace orders (Amazon/Flipkart) for IC-1800 wall-point service follow the respective marketplace's fee refund policy; Dhruva processes refunds only for direct orders.
