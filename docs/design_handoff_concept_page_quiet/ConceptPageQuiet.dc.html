How is churn rate (weekly, per cohort) computed, end to end?
4 published concepts · high coverage

Churn is computed weekly over cohortstrm.cohort of active userstrm.active. The WAU pipeline joins events → usersjoin.events_users, excludes rows where status = 'deleted'tbl.users, and divides users lost this week by the cohort's prior-week countmet.churn.
