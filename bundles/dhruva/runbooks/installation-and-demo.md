**RO-7:** installation auto-schedules within **48 h of delivery** in installable
pincodes (a subset of the deliverable zones in [[shipping-serviceability]] — never
promise installation from deliverability alone). Free standard kit includes wall
mounting, up to 3 m food-grade tubing, one tap-diverter valve, and commissioning
with a TDS demo reading. Chargeable extras, payable to the technician against a
digital receipt: extra tubing ₹60/m, booster pump for pressure < 0.3 kg/cm²
(₹2,950), pre-softener advisory for TDS > 1500 ppm. Self- or plumber-installed
units lose leak-related coverage per [[warranty-policy]].

**IC-1800:** no installation required; an optional 16 A wall-point service can be
added at checkout in metro pincodes (₹349, includes point testing). The demo covers
pan compatibility and the E-code card.

Reschedules: up to 2 at no charge via the SR link; a failed visit due to site not
ready (no drilling permission, no water point) converts the visit to chargeable
(₹199) on the next booking.
