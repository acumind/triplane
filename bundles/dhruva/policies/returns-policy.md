**10-day return window** from delivery date for change-of-mind returns, refunded to
source payment method (COD refunds to UPI/bank, 5–7 business days after pickup QC).

| Case | Window | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Change of mind (unused, all packaging) | 10 days | Refund, two-way pickup free |
| Damaged in transit | **48 h** to report | Replacement priority |
| Dead on arrival (DOA) | 7 days | Replacement (not repair) |
| Wrong item shipped | 10 days | Replacement or refund |

**Evidence requirements:** transit-damage and DOA claims require the **unboxing
video** or photos of the outer carton with the shipping label visible. Claims
without either go to service-center inspection instead of doorstep approval, adding
3–5 business days.

**Category exclusions:** opened RO consumables (filters, membranes, copper
cartridges) are non-returnable on hygiene grounds; jar sets with mounted blades used
even once are return-ineligible but remain covered by the 6-month jar tier in
[[warranty-policy]]. Marketplace orders (Amazon/Flipkart) follow the marketplace's
window for returns, but warranty service always comes to Dhruva directly with the
marketplace [[invoice-required]].

Refund of any bundled [[dhruva-shield]] plan is pro-rata only if the base product is
returned within the 10-day window.
