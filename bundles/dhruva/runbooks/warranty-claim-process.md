1. **Pre-check** the product page's self-help (MG OTP cool-down, IC E-code table,
   RO filter indicator) — roughly a third of claims resolve here.
2. **Book an SR** via 1800-419-xxxx (9:00–21:00 IST), WhatsApp, or the storefront
   service page. Keep the serial number and [[invoice-required]] ready; the SR
   number is issued immediately and is the reference for everything after.
3. **Triage & mode:** doorstep technician (Zone A/B) or nearest
   [[authorized-service-center]] carry-in (Zone C and all jar-program requests).
   DOA cases (≤ 7 days) skip repair triage and go straight to replacement approval.
4. **Inspection outcome** is recorded against the SR as covered / excluded /
   chargeable-estimate. IC-1800 over-voltage disputes are decided by the board's
   fault log; RO leak claims are decided by installation record + cartridge check.
5. **Service levels:** doorstep first visit ≤ 2 business days (metro) / ≤ 4 (Zone B);
   repair TAT 5 business days if parts in stock, +3–5 for part indent. Breach of TAT
   auto-escalates the SR; 10+ days triggers replacement review.
6. **Closure:** OTP-confirmed with the customer; repaired-part workmanship carries a
   90-day guarantee. Escalations: sr-escalations@ email or WhatsApp "ESCALATE <SR>".

Out-of-warranty path: the same SR flow returns a chargeable estimate; the
40%-of-MRP jar program in [[warranty-policy]] is requested as SR type "JAR-OOW".
