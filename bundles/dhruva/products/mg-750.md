750 W mixer grinder for Indian kitchens — wet grinding, dry grinding, and chutney
duty in a single unit. ISI-marked motor, 5-year-rated nylon couplers.


**Duty cycle:** run ≤ 5 minutes continuously, then rest 2 minutes. Idli/dosa batter:
soak rice ≥ 4 h and grind in 2 batches for the 1.5 L jar. Continuous commercial duty
(restaurants, cloud kitchens, tiffin services) is outside intended use — see the
commercial-use exclusion in [[warranty-policy]].

**Common issues before raising a claim:** if the unit stops mid-grind, it is usually
the OTP, not a failure — let it cool 20 minutes. A burning smell in the first two
uses is coupler run-in and normal; persistent smell after that is claim-worthy through the standard service flow.

Compatible spare jars and what does **not** fit (MG-500 jars do not): see
[[mg750-jar-compatibility]] and the [[jar-set-pro]] accessory.
