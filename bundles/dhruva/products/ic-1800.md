1800 W induction cooktop with preset Indian menus (dal, dosa, deep-fry, milk) and a
crystal-glass top rated to 8 kg static load.


**Voltage reality:** SmartSense derates output below 190 V instead of shutting down,
so cooking slows during brown-outs rather than stopping. Sustained supply **above
270 V** (common with faulty inverters or phase faults) can damage the IGBT board and
is an exclusion in [[warranty-policy]] unless a stabilizer or the surge condition is
evidenced. Error **E7** indicates over-voltage shutdown — note it in any claim; the
board logs the last 5 fault events and the [[authorized-service-center]] reads them
during inspection.

**E-code quick guide:** E1 no pan / wrong pan · E3 low voltage · E5 overheat (let it
cool, check ventilation gap ≥ 10 cm) · E7 over-voltage · E8 sensor fault (claim).

No installation needed; see [[installation-and-demo]] only if purchased with the
optional wall-point service.
