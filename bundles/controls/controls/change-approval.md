No change reaches a production ledger system without a recorded approval from someone other than its author.

Segregation of duties is the whole point: the approver must not be the author. The [[release-runbook]] is the operating procedure, and its approval record is the evidence.

Approvals are captured in the same system that performs the release, so the evidence cannot be produced after the fact.
