Revenue recognised in a period must relate to goods or services delivered in that period.

Testing samples invoices either side of the period boundary and traces each to its delivery record. A mismatch is a cut-off error; a pattern of mismatches is a deficiency in [[operating-effectiveness]].

Sampled invoices carry customer identifiers, so the working papers are bound by [[data-retention]].
