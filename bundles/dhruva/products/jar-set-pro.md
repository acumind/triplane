Replacement/expansion 3-jar pack for the MG-7xx platform: 1.5 L wet jar with
flow-breaker, 1.0 L dry jar, 0.4 L chutney jar. Uses the same WG-series blade
assembly and lid-lock geometry as the jars shipped with [[mg-750]].

Sold as an accessory SKU (DH-JSP-3). Jar-only purchases carry the **6-month jar
warranty tier** described in [[warranty-policy]] — the 24-month motor tier never
applies to accessory jars. Fitment across MG-500 / MG-650 / MG-750 / MG-900 bodies
is defined only by [[mg750-jar-compatibility]]; do not infer fitment from wattage
or from listing photos.
