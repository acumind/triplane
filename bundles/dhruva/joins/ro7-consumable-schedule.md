Consumables are excluded from warranty per [[warranty-policy]]; intervals below
assume municipal water at ~500 ppm TDS. High-TDS borewell input (1200–2000 ppm)
roughly **halves** membrane and pre-filter life.

| Consumable | SKU | Replace every | Symptom when overdue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sediment pre-filter | DH-RF-S | 6 months | Slow flow, pump noise |
| Pre-carbon | DH-RF-C1 | 6 months | Chlorine taste |
| RO membrane (75 GPD) | DH-RM-75 | 12–18 months | TDS creep, tank fills slowly |
| Post-carbon + mineralizer | DH-RF-C2M | 12 months | Flat taste |
| Copper cartridge | DH-CU-1 | 12 months | Copper indicator on display |
| UV lamp | DH-UV-11 | 24 months | UV-fail alarm |

Only DH-series cartridges keep the leak coverage in [[warranty-policy]] intact —
generic cartridges physically fit but their o-ring spec differs, and leak claims
with third-party cartridges installed are rejected on inspection. AquaCare
([[dhruva-shield]]) covers this entire table for its plan year.
