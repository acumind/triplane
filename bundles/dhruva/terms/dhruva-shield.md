Paid plans purchasable within 30 days of the product invoice:

| Plan | Applies to | Adds |
|---|---|---|
| Shield+1 / Shield+2 | MG-7xx, IC-1800 | +12 / +24 months on the **motor/electrical tier only** |
| AquaCare | RO-7 | 1 year: all consumables + 2 scheduled services + priority SLA |

Shield never extends the 6-month jar tier, never covers consumables outside
AquaCare, and inherits every exclusion in [[warranty-policy]]. Claims flow through
the same [[warranty-claim-process]]; the SR system applies Shield automatically when
the serial has an active plan. Shield is refundable pro-rata only alongside a
base-product return.
