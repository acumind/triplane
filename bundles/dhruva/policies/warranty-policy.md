Coverage is from the **invoice date** (see [[invoice-required]]), against
manufacturing defects only, serviced through the [[authorized-service-center]]
network via [[warranty-claim-process]]. Warranty is on the product, not the buyer —
it survives gifting but not commercial deployment.

## Coverage matrix

| Product / part | Coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MG-7xx motor & electricals | 24 months | Copper winding, switch, OTP, coupler |
| **Jars, blades, lids (incl. accessory jars)** | **6 months** | Manufacturing defects only |
| IC-1800 (complete unit) | 24 months | Glass top: transit damage only, 48 h window |
| RO-7 electrical system | 24 months | Pump, SMPS, UV ballast, solenoid, float |
| RO-7 tank & body plastics | 12 months | Discoloration is not a defect |
| **RO consumables** (filters, membrane, copper cartridge) | **Not covered** | See [[ro7-consumable-schedule]] |

## The jar clause, precisely

A cracked, chipped, or leaking jar is covered **only if both** conditions hold:
within **6 months** of invoice, **and** the damage is a manufacturing defect
(spontaneous crack lines, weld/seam failure, blade-shaft wobble). Impact damage,
drops, dishwasher warping, and grinding of hot ingredients are excluded at any age.

**Out-of-warranty jar program:** beyond 6 months, Dhruva offers replacement jars at
**40% of prevailing MRP** (one jar per product serial per year) through any
[[authorized-service-center]], against the original [[invoice-required]]. This is a
goodwill program, not a warranty extension, and does not reset any warranty clock.

## Standard exclusions (all products)

Physical/impact/liquid damage · commercial use (restaurants, cloud kitchens, PGs,
offices) · unauthorized repair or opening (voids remaining coverage — see
[[authorized-service-center]]) · missing or mismatched serial number · rodent/insect
damage · cosmetic wear · **IC-1800**: sustained input above 270 V without stabilizer
evidence (board fault log is decisive; error E7 supports the claim) · **RO-7**:
input TDS above 2000 ppm without pre-softener, or non-Dhruva installation
(leak-related claims only).

## Interplay with extended coverage

[[dhruva-shield]] extends the *motor/electrical* tiers only; the 6-month jar tier
and the consumables exclusion are never extended by any plan. DOA units (dead on
arrival, reported within 7 days) are replaced, not repaired — see
[[warranty-claim-process]].
